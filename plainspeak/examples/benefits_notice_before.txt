NOTICE OF ADVERSE ACTION
Re: Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program

Dear Program Participant,

Pursuant to the regulations governing the Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program, you are hereby notified that a determination has been made by this agency to terminate your household's benefits effective at the conclusion of the current certification period, due to the fact that verification of household composition and monetary income, which was requested in correspondence previously transmitted to the address of record, was not received by the agency prior to the established due date.

In the event that the beneficiary is in disagreement with the aforementioned determination, a fair hearing may be requested in accordance with applicable state and federal regulations. Requests for a fair hearing shall be submitted in writing to the appropriate local office not later than ninety days subsequent to the date of this notice.

Failure to furnish sufficient documentation may result in the termination of benefits and the establishment of an overpayment claim, recoupment of which will be initiated in accordance with program requirements. Additional assistance pertaining to this matter may be obtained by contacting the agency utilizing the information provided herein.
