Pre-ticked consentCD01 · Sneaking · line 23 · input[name=news]

Send me Leafbox offers and share my details with partners

Why it matters. Consent you didn't give is not consent. Pre-ticked boxes quietly sign people up for marketing or data sharing they never chose.

Fix. Leave every consent checkbox unticked by default and let people opt in.

GDPR Recital 32 & Art. 7 · CJEU Planet49 (C-673/17, 2019) · CCPA Regs, 11 CCR §7004

Sneak into basketCD02 · Sneaking · line 24 · input[name=protect]

Add Delivery Protection Plan (+$3.99/box)

Why it matters. Paid extras that are pre-selected end up in the basket unless the shopper notices and removes them.

Fix. Offer extras unticked; only add them to the order after an explicit choice.

EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU, Art. 22 · FTC Act §5

Hidden subscription termsCD04 · Forced continuity · line 30 · p

Your membership renews automatically at $89.99/month after the 7-day trial until cancelled. To cancel, you must call our member services line during business h…

Why it matters. Burying auto-renewal or post-trial charges in fine print turns a 'free trial' into a subscription people didn't knowingly agree to.

Fix. State the price, billing frequency and renewal date clearly next to the sign-up button, at the same size as the offer.

ROSCA, 15 U.S.C. §8403 · California Automatic Renewal Law, Bus. & Prof. Code §17600 et seq.

Asymmetric consentCD10 · Obstruction · line 36 · div#cookie-banner

'Accept all' offered with no equally easy way to refuse

Why it matters. When 'Accept' is one bright click and 'Reject' is hidden, tiny or missing, the banner manufactures consent rather than asking for it.

Fix. Put 'Reject all' on the first layer, with the same size, style and number of clicks as 'Accept all'.

ePrivacy Directive Art. 5(3) & GDPR Art. 7(3) · EDPB Cookie Banner Taskforce report (2023) · CNIL sanctions against Google & Meta (2021-22)

False urgencyCD06 · Urgency · line 16 · div.banner

Hurry! Your 60% discount ends in 09:59

Why it matters. Countdown timers and 'ends soon' banners pressure people into deciding before they can compare or think. They are often fake and reset on reload.

Fix. Only show deadlines that are real, and state the actual end date.

EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, Annex I point 7 · FTC, Bringing Dark Patterns to Light (2022)

Drip pricingCD03 · Sneaking · line 19 · small

+ service fee

Why it matters. Advertising a low price and revealing mandatory fees later makes honest comparison impossible and exploits sunk-cost at checkout.

Fix. Show the total price, including mandatory fees, wherever a price is shown.

FTC Rule on Unfair or Deceptive Fees, 16 CFR Part 464 (2025) · California SB 478 (Honest Pricing Law, 2024)

Trick questionCD11 · Misdirection · line 25 · input[name=sms]

Uncheck this box if you do not want to not receive SMS updates

Why it matters. Double negatives and inverted checkboxes ('untick to not receive') make people choose the opposite of what they intend.

Fix. Phrase every option positively: 'Send me offers by email' with an unticked box.

CCPA Regs, 11 CCR §7004 · FTC, Bringing Dark Patterns to Light (2022)

ConfirmshamingCD09 · Misdirection · line 28 · a.decline

No thanks, I prefer paying full price for sad food

Why it matters. Wording the 'no' option as a self-insult guilts people into accepting.

Fix. Use neutral labels for every choice, e.g. 'No thanks'.

FTC, Bringing Dark Patterns to Light (2022) · EU Digital Services Act, Art. 25

Obstructed cancellationCD05 · Obstruction · line 30 · p

Your membership renews automatically at $89.99/month after the 7-day trial until cancelled. To cancel, you must call our member services line during business h…

Why it matters. Easy to join, hard to leave: forcing a phone call or letter to cancel keeps people paying for things they no longer want.

Fix. Let people cancel the same way they signed up, online, in a few clicks.

Activity notificationsCD08 · Social proof · line 34 · div.toast

Priya in Austin just bought the Family Plan

Why it matters. Pop-ups like 'Sarah in Leeds just bought this' simulate popularity and are frequently fabricated.

Fix. Remove simulated activity feeds; if you show real activity, make it verifiable and aggregate.

EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, Art. 6 (misleading actions) · Mathur et al., Dark Patterns at Scale (2019)

Scarcity pressureCD07 · Scarcity · line 20 · p.stock

Only 3 left at this price · 14 people are viewing this right now

Why it matters. Low-stock and high-demand messages push people to buy now. They are legal when true, but often generated without real inventory data.

Fix. Show stock levels only when they come from live inventory, and avoid manufactured 'people are looking' counters.

EU Unfair Commercial Practices Directive, Art. 6 · Mathur et al., Dark Patterns at Scale (2019)
