# EthicalAI

Small, transparent AI projects aimed at real-world harms. Each one is lightweight, explainable and privacy-respecting by default.

| Project | Problem | Approach | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| [🚩 **RedFlag**](redflag/) | Text, email and phone scams cost people billions a year, and older adults are hit hardest. | Offline, glass-box scam detector: 13 readable red-flag signals, weights learned by logistic regression, and plain-language advice for each flag. Zero dependencies. | v0.1 |

## Principles

- **Explainable over impressive.** If a person can't understand why the model said something, the model isn't done.
- **Private by default.** Runs locally and collects nothing.
- **Honest evaluation.** Report held-out numbers and known failures.
- **Built for the people affected,** not only for engineers.
