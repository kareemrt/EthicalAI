Millbrook County Health Department - Lead in Drinking Water Advisory

There is no known safe level of lead exposure for children. Young children and pregnant people face the greatest risk. Households with lead service lines should run cold water for at least two minutes before drinking if water has been sitting for several hours. Boiling water does not remove lead. Certified filters labeled for lead reduction can lower lead levels. The department offers free blood lead testing for children under six at its Main Street clinic.
