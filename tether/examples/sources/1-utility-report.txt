Millbrook Water Utility - 2025 Service Line Inventory

The utility completed its inventory of 18,412 service lines in March 2025. Of these, 2,317 lines were confirmed to contain lead and a further 4,105 were classified as unknown material. Replacement work began in the Eastside district, where older housing stock is concentrated. The utility replaced 640 lead lines during 2025 at an average cost of $8,900 per line. Federal infrastructure grants covered 70% of replacement costs. Residents are not required to pay for replacement of the public portion of the line. The utility aims to replace all confirmed lead lines by 2033.
