Here is a summary of Millbrook's lead pipe situation:

- Millbrook has inventoried 18,412 service lines, and 2,317 of them were confirmed to contain lead [1].
- Replacement work started in the Eastside district, where older housing is concentrated [1].
- In 2025 the utility replaced 640 lead lines at an average cost of $9,800 per line [1].
- Federal infrastructure grants covered 70% of replacement costs [2].
- The utility has promised to "replace every lead line by 2030" [1].
- Boiling water removes lead, so families should boil tap water before drinking it [2].
- Certified filters labeled for lead reduction can lower lead levels [2].
- The health department offers free blood lead testing for all residents.
- Lead service lines were banned nationwide in 1986.
