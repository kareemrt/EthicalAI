How to read this

  • Impact ratio = group selection rate ÷ the highest group's rate. Below 0.8 (the EEOC four-fifths rule, also used by NYC Local Law 144) indicates potential adverse impact.
  • ADVERSE IMPACT means the ratio is below 0.8 and the gap is statistically significant (two-proportion z-test, p < 0.05). REVIEW means below 0.8 but not significant — often a small-sample signal worth investigating.
  • Groups under 2.0% of the data, and records with an unknown category, are shown but excluded from ratios.
  • When ground-truth labels are supplied, TPR (share of qualified people who were selected) and FPR (share of unqualified people selected) reveal whether errors fall unevenly across groups.
  • A passing audit is evidence, not proof, of fairness. Pair it with domain review, data provenance checks and the voices of affected people.
