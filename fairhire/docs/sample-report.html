Method

  • **Selection rate** — share of a group's applicants the tool advanced.
  • **Impact ratio** — a group's selection rate divided by the highest group's rate. Below 0.80 is the EEOC "four-fifths rule" signal of adverse impact; NYC Local Law 144 requires these ratios to be published.
  • **p-value** — two-proportion z-test against the reference group; below 0.05 means the gap is unlikely to be chance.
  • **TPR / FPR** — when ground-truth qualification is known: the share of qualified (TPR) and unqualified (FPR) applicants who were advanced. Unequal TPR means qualified people in some groups are being missed.
  • Groups under the minimum share of the data are reported but not scored. *Not scored is not the same as fair.* This report is a statistical screen, not a legal determination.
